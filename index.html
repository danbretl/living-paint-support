Paint that is alive.

In Living Paint, every dot of color is a tiny creature. It eats, has young, wanders, fights at its borders, and now and then is born a brand-new color. Red fights, green grows and blue wanders, so the color you choose decides how your paint behaves.

Pick any color and paint. Feed your creatures, build walls to keep rivals apart, and let time run faster or slower. Look at the same painting eight ways, start from a garden, a blank canvas or one of your photos, and save a picture or a timelapse of the painting’s whole life.

Living Paint is in beta for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Vision Pro. It began as a college thesis in 2006 and 2007 and was rebuilt in 2026.

Your paintings stay on your device. There’s no Living Paint account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the app doesn’t connect to the internet. The privacy policy explains exactly what the app keeps and where.

Questions

Ask in public GitHub issues. Posting requires a GitHub account, and everything you post there is public, so never include a picture or painting you’d rather keep private.
