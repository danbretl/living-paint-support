Living Paint Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Living Paint is a painting program in which the paint is alive. This policy describes Living Paint for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Vision Pro.

The short version

Living Paint keeps your painting on your device. There’s no Living Paint account or server, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the app doesn’t connect to the internet. Living Paint never sends your paintings, pictures or photos to the developer.

What Living Paint keeps on your device

Living Paint stores these in its private app storage, which other apps can’t read:

  • your current painting: its living creatures, colors, food, walls and paint, and the settings it runs with, saved automatically so it resumes where you left it;
  • a timelapse of that painting’s life: a few hundred small pictures of the canvas over time, kept beside the painting and deleted when you start a new one;
  • exact copies of any saved painting the app couldn’t read, so a damaged save never erases your work;
  • a few preferences, such as which view of the canvas you chose.

Photos and pictures

When you start a painting From a Photo, you choose one picture in the system photo picker or, on a Mac, in an Open window. Living Paint can see only the picture you choose. It reduces that picture to the canvas’s colors, and those colors become the start of your painting and its timelapse. The original photo isn’t copied, kept or changed.

Save Picture and Timelapse can add a picture, video or animated GIF to your photo library when you choose Save to Photos. Living Paint asks only to add to your library and never reads it. Share sends what you made only to the place you choose in the share sheet, and from there that place’s own terms apply.

Files

Save a Copy, Open Painting and the Lab’s research export read or write only the files and folders you choose. A saved painting or export leaves Living Paint only for the place you pick.

Backups

Your device’s backups can include what Living Paint keeps on the device. Depending on your settings, that includes iCloud Backup, a backup to a computer, and Time Machine on a Mac. Apple’s backup settings, including Advanced Data Protection for iCloud, decide how a backup is protected.

On a Mac and Apple Vision Pro

On a Mac, Living Paint runs in Apple’s App Sandbox and can read and write only its own storage and files you choose. It has no network access. On Apple Vision Pro, Living Paint shows its canvas in a window and can show the living creatures around you; it doesn’t use the cameras or track your surroundings or hands.

What Living Paint doesn’t do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No internet connection, server or sync.
  • No ads, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools, and no third-party code that collects data.
  • No selling or sharing of your information.
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera or microphone, and no reading of your photo library.

Apple may give the developer statistics such as download counts, and crash reports from people who chose to share analytics with app developers. If you test Living Paint through TestFlight, feedback you choose to send, including screenshots, goes to the developer through Apple; never send a screenshot that shows something you’d rather keep private. Apple provides these under its own privacy policy.

Keeping and deleting your paintings

Your painting stays on your device until you replace it with a new painting or delete the app. Starting a new painting replaces the current one and its timelapse; use Save a Copy first to keep it. Deleting the app from iPhone, iPad or Apple Vision Pro removes its storage; on a Mac, moving the app to the Trash leaves its data in your user Library. Copies, exports, pictures and videos you saved elsewhere follow their own storage settings. Living Paint never sends your paintings to the developer, so there’s nothing for the developer to delete on a server.

This website and questions

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting it is separate from using Living Paint. GitHub may process your IP address, browser or device information and request details to operate its hosting. The site has no ads, analytics or scripts. GitHub explains how it handles website and account information in its Privacy Statement.

Questions go through public GitHub issues. Posting requires a GitHub account, and issues, your GitHub username and any attachments are public. Never post a picture or painting you’d rather keep private. The developer reads what you post in order to answer it. To have an issue removed, ask in that issue without repeating the information you want removed.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will appear at this address with a new date. Living Paint would describe any change that sends your paintings anywhere new here before it shipped.

Contact

Living Paint is made by Dan Bretl. For questions about this policy, open an issue at github.com/danbretl/living-paint-support/issues.
